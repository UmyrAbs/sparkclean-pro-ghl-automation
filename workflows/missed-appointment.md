# Missed Appointment Workflow

## Purpose
Help SparkClean Pro follow up with contacts who miss a scheduled consultation.

## Trigger
Appointment Status No Show for the Free Consultation calendar.

## Action Sequence
1. Add a note or record the missed appointment, according to the configured action.
2. Send an internal notification to SparkClean Admin.
3. Send a rescheduling SMS.
4. Send a rescheduling email.
5. Create a follow up task assigned to SparkClean Admin.

## Testing
Tested by marking a test appointment as No Show. The rescheduling email was received successfully.

## Notes
SMS delivery depends on the messaging number and account configuration.
