# New Lead Nurture Workflow

## Purpose
Automate initial follow up for new SparkClean Pro leads.

## Trigger
Contact Created.

## Action Sequence
1. Apply the New Lead tag.
2. Wait 2 minutes.
3. Send the welcome email.
4. Wait 10 minutes.
5. Send the follow up SMS.
6. Create or update the opportunity.
7. Assign the contact to SparkClean Admin.
8. Send an internal notification.
9. Continue with follow up email and SMS.
10. Create a follow up task.

## Testing
Tested through the lead capture process. Contact creation, tagging, opportunity creation, and welcome email delivery were verified.
